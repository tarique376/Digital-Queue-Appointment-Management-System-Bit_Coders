# Submission package

The source PDF requires a short demonstration video, at least one GitHub/deployed access option, and a separate PDF/Word explanation.

## Recording sequence

1. Show customer dashboard, departments, and services.
2. Book an available appointment; show reference, notification, and history.
3. Check in within the configured window and show the linked token/eligibility.
4. Use another customer to get a walk-in token; show people ahead and waiting estimate.
5. Switch to staff: assigned counter, mixed queue, Call Next, recall/skip where appropriate, Start, Complete.
6. Show public display and customer status updating within the five-second refresh.
7. Pause an available counter; show revised estimates or unavailable capacity.
8. Show manager/admin schedules, assignments, capacity, closures, reports, and activity log.
9. Show rescheduling/cancellation and explain optional SMTP/worker operation.
10. Finish with source/deployed access and explanation document. Do not claim an unimplemented AI model.

Use private separate sessions and an appointment near current service time. Do not record `.env.local`, passwords, or provider secrets. PROJECT_EXPLANATION.md identifies the implementation and can be exported to PDF/Word. Publish only to the user's selected account/environment, excluding credentials, local databases, builds, and test output.
